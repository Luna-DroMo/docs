## Overview
The **Methods Center (Methodenzentrum)** at the University of Tübingen links quantitative statistics, psychometrics, and qualitative/mixed-methods to tackle substantive problems in education, psychology, economics and health.  
Founded as an independent institute in 2020 within the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences, it currently hosts ~30 researchers across three departments.

## Main Research Lines
### 1. Dynamic & Non-normal Latent-Variable Modeling
The Quantitative Methods team develops state-space and dynamic structural equation models for intensive longitudinal data, incorporating Bayesian adaptive Lasso and horseshoe+ priors.

### 2. Psychometrics & Measurement
Under Prof. Holger Brandt, the Psychometrics unit combines machine learning with Item-Response Theory to improve high-frequency assessment and computerized adaptive testing.

### 3. Qualitative & Mixed-Methods Innovation
Researchers adapt grounded theory, situational analysis, and Qualitative Comparative Analysis (QCA) for digital fieldwork and large multi-case projects, offering consulting and annual spring schools.

## Teaching & Outreach
- **M.Sc. Quantitative Data Science Methods** covers psychometrics, econometrics and ML, attracting ≈40 students per cohort.  
- Open spring/fall schools and workshops reach >300 participants annually; the 2025 fall school will focus on Bayesian causal inference.  
- The center offers a campus-wide methods consulting service for DFG/BMBF-funded projects.
